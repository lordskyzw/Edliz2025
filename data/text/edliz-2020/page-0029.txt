> Gloves should not replace hand hygiene
Gloves are to be worn when touching the following:

e Blood

e ~All body fluids

e All body secretions

e All body excretions

Gloves should be removed before touching clean items (e.g. phone, door knobs
or patients’ charts.) Perform hand hygiene after removing gloves.
Important points to remember when using:

a) Gloves.

e Use gloves when there is potential exposure to blood, body fluids,
excretions, or secretions.

e Change gloves between patients, between procedures on the same
patient when they become soiled.

e Remove gloves before leaving the patient's bedside and
decontaminate hands immediately with 70% alcohol hand rub
solution.

e Discard gloves after attending to each patient.

b) Boots/shoe covers

e These are used to protect the wearer from splashes of blood, body
fluids, secretions, and excretions.

e Shoe covers should be disposable and waterproof.

e Waterproof boots should be washable.

c) Caps

e Disposable and waterproof caps that completely cover the hair are

used when splashes of blood and body fluids are expected.
