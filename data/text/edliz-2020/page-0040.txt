e — Any case of pneumonia should be considered as a potential COVID-
19 case and have that possibility excluded through checking for risk
factors i.e. have they travelled, have they been exposed to a COVID-
19 case etc.

Who should be admitted?

e Assess the patient.

e — If mild symptoms can go home

e If having difficulty breathing, O2 saturation <90, arrange for
admission,

e Make sure that you have excluded other possible causes of fever and
shortness of breath e.g. PCP, malaria, bacterial pneumonia,
pulmonary embolism etc.

MANAGING THE CASE:

e Treat as we would normally do for a case of pneumonia as per
EDLIZ guidelines

e Give empiric antibiotics to treat all likely pathogens causing
pneumonia e.g. in most patients unless they have documented
allergies give:

Ceftriaxone 2g iv start followed by 1g bd iv x 1/52 &

Azithromycin 500mg od po on day 1 and then 250mg daily

x 4 days

e  Forchildren -use Ceftriaxone 50-80mg/kg plus Azithromycin 10mg/kg
e Paracetamol 500mg -1gm every 6 -8 hours as required for fever

e — Give patient a surgical mask if you suspect COVID-19. Wear PPE
yourself.

e Give supplemental oxygen therapy immediately to patients with
respiratory distress, hypoxaemia, or shock,

e — Avoid overloading the patient with intravenous fluids. Patients should
be treated cautiously with intravenous fluids, because aggressive
fluid resuscitation may worsen oxygenation, especially in settings
where there is limited availability of mechanical ventilation,
