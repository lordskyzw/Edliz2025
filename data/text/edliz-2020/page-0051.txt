Breastmilk Fortification
e¢  Breastmilk fortification helps to meet the increased energy and
mineral requirements of preterm infants.
e — Fortify breastmilk of preterm infants < 1500g or < 32 weeks with
FM85 when they are tolerating 160mls/kg/day of EBM.
e Add 0.5g FM85 to bolus feeds of s 24mls and 1g per bolus feed =
25mls.
e If breastmilk is fortified a total volume of 160mls/kg/day will suffice to
meet energy requirements.
