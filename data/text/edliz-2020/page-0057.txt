Benzodiazepines
These may be required in 15-20% of neonatal seizures. Lorazepam:
0.05mg/kg iv bolus over 2-5 minutes (may be repeated)
Midazolam: 0.15mg/kg iv bolus followed by an infusion of 0.1-0.4mg/kg/hr
*Avoid diazepam in neonatal seizures because of its short duration of anti-
epileptic effect and its very prolonged sedative effect.
