Supportive measures
= Prevent low blood sugar:
= If the child is able to breastfeed ask the mother to breast feed
the child
= If the child cannot breastfeed but is able to swallow give
expressed breast milk or a breast milk substitute. If neither are
available give sugar water = 4 level teaspoons sugar (20gm) in
200ml clean water.
= If the child is not able to swallow, give 50m! of milk or sugar
water by nasogastric tube.
= — Fluids (po/iv/nasogastric) 100ml/kg/24hrs - iv fluids monitored
closely
= Nasal suction (or normal saline nasal drops) to clear the
airway.
= Continued feeding.
= Check oxygen saturation
= Give Oxygen.
