Oral Rehydration Solution: Full Formula has now
been replaced with low osmolarity ORS formula.
It has low levels of glucose and salt to achieve osmolarity of 245mOsm/L
resulting in improved efficacy and decreased stool output. It is safe and
effective even in children with cholera.
