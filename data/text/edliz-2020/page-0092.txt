Home management

e Regularly monitor child’s weight: For children less than 1year old
monitor weight every month and for those 1year and above, check
weight every 2 months.

e Encourage exclusive breastfeeding up to 6 months (no additional
fluids/foods), introducing other foods in addition to breast milk at 6
months, and breastfeeding up to two years. For young children,
continue breastfeeding on demand.

e _RUTF is a food and medicine for children with Severe acute
malnutrition. (SAM)- oedematous and non oedematous
(Kwashiorkor or Marasmus) only. It should not be shared.

e = Children with SAM (Kwashiorkor or Marasmus) often do not like to
eat. Give small regular meals of RUTF and encourage the child to
eat often (if possible eight meals a day).

e Always offer the child plenty of cooled, boiled water to drink while
he or she is eating RUTF.

e Keep food clean and covered. Hygienic food handling and
preparation. Use soap to wash children’s hands and face before
feeding. Wash hands with soap and water after visiting the toilet.

e = Children with SAM (Kwashiorkor or Marasmus) get cold quickly.
Always keep the child covered and warm.

e When a child has diarrhoea, never stop feeding. Give extra food
and extra clean water.

As soon as the child gets better, introduce the fortified, family type diet.
