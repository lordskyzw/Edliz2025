Notes on Paediatric Medicines Doses:
. for infants under one month see Tables 4.1,4.2, 4.3 and 4.5;
. read the “dosage” column carefully in conjunction with the
“doses/day” column;
. do not exceed the adult dose: the dosage per kg is not
applicable for children > 40kg;
Table 4.16: Dosages for Children and Infants Over 2 Months
For antibiotics where “Doses/Day” give a range (e.g. 2-4) the
number of doses/day should be chosen according to both the
baby’s gestational age and postnatal age. Do not exceed the
frequency recommended in the table.
