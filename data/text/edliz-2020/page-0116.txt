Acute Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID)
Acute PID refers to the acute syndrome attributed to the ascent of
microorganisms, not related to pregnancy or surgery, from the vagina and cervix
to the endometrium, fallopian tubes and adnexal structures. Gonorrhoea,
chlamydia, mycoplasma, anaerobic bacteria and gram-negative organisms can
cause acute PID.
