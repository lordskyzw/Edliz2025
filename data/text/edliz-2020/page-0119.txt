CAUTION: Iron preparations should be taken after food to avoid
gastrointestinal irritation. If vomiting occurs, reduce dosage to that which
can be tolerated.
Severe anaemia in pregnancy requires full investigation:
* — stool for ova and parasites;
= peripheral blood film for malarial parasites;
* — full blood count;
*  mid-stream specimen of urine for microscopy, culture and sensitivity;
* and HIV test.
Severe anaemia (Hb < 8 gms) after 36 weeks of gestation requires admission
and possible transfusion, as well as oral iron therapy. (See the chapter on
Haematology and blood products). Parenteral iron preparations can be used if
Hb is <7g/dl.
