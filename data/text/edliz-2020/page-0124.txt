Use of steroids in pre-term labour

Steroids are used to prevent respiratory distress syndrome of the newborn in

premature labour before 35 weeks gestation. Most useful between 28-35 weeks

gestation.

Cervical ripeners/ labour initiators (Prostaglandins)

Use prostaglandins (PG) with caution in multiparous women. Excessive

uterine contractions can lead to uterine rupture, particularly if the cervix is

not ripe.
