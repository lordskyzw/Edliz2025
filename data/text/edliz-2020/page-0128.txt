Repeat doses of misoprostol can be considered when needed to achieve
success of the abortion process. Health-care providers should use caution and
clinical judgement to decide the maximum number of doses of misoprostol
when managing pregnant individuals with prior uterine incision. Uterine rupture
though a rare complication, must be considered with advanced gestational age
and therefore clinical judgement and health system preparedness for
emergency management should be made available.
