OPHTHALMIA NEONATORUM
This is defined as conjunctivitis with discharge occurring in a neonate within the
first month of life. The condition is commonly caused by gonococcal, chlamydial
and bacterial infection. The condition is preventable by detecting and treating
maternal gonococcal and chlamydial infection during pregnancy and by instilling
1% tetracycline eye ointment carefully into the conjunctival sacs of every baby
as soon as possible after birth.
