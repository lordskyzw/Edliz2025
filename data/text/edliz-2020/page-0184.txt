Monitoring children on ART
e Check haemoglobin after at least 6-8 weeks if on Zidovudine
e Use urine dipsticks to check for glycosuria and estimated
glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) and/or serum creatinine when on
Tenofovir
e Check for alanine aminotransferase (ALT) when on Nevirapine
e Check CD4 count every 6 months
e Conduct viral load testing once every year or when clinical signs
are suggestive of treatment failure
