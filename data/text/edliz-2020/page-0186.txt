Starting ART in children using FDCs
Refer to dosing table. Keep the following factors in mind regarding dosing:

e Medicine doses must be adjusted as the child grows.

e Dosing is by weight.

° Overdosing up to 10% is acceptable.

e Scored tablets may be divided into two equal halves

e Tablets may be crushed and mixed with a small amount food or

water and administered immediately.
