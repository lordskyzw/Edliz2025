¢ BCG vaccine should be given to all babies at birth or at first contact with the
child after birth, except babies with clinical signs of HIV infection and/or in
infants born to a mother with sputum positive TB.
e BCG is given intradermally on the right upper arm, above the insertion of
the deltoid muscle.
e The batch number of the vaccine and the date must be recorded on the
child's health card.
e No booster dose should be given.
Dosage is as recommended by the EPI (see the chapter on Immunisation).
Problems associated with BCG vaccination remain uncommon and are mainly
due to faulty technique.
Abscesses or ulcers should be treated with local hygienic care. Abscesses
should be aspirated not incised. Secondary infections can be treated with
antibiotics. Non-healing ulcers, (ulcers of duration > 8 weeks) or regional
lymphadenopathy can be treated as follows, (see table10. 1).
