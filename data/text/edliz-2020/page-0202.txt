TUBERCULOSIS CASE MANAGEMENT
Diagnosis of TB
The presence of pulmonary tuberculosis should be suspected in
individuals presenting with one or more of the following complaints:
e Cough for 1 week or longer,
e Night sweats,
e ©Fever,
e Loss of weight,
¢ aBMI <17kg/m* or failure to thrive among children.
Laboratory Investigations
Every effort should be made to bacteriologically confirm the diagnosis
of TB, with rapid molecular test such as the Xpert MTB/Rif test being
the preferred diagnostic test for patients being evaluated for
tuberculosis. A specimen should be submitted for all cases being
investigated for pulmonary TB as per TB diagnostic algorithm, in
order to confirm the diagnosis of Tuberculosis. (see the National
Tuberculosis and Leprosy Control Guidelines)
