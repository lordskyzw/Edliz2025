TREATMENT REGIMENS FOR TB
One treatment regimen is now used in Zimbabwe for the treatment of
drug sensitive TB, regardless of the treatment history. The regimen
consists of a combination of the four first line medicines HRZE:
H= isoniazid E= ethambutol
R= rifampicin Z= pyrazinamide

These medicines are available as Fixed Dose Combination (FDC).
See tables on the next page for dosing.
The intention of these combination tablets is to improve compliance
by reducing the number of tablets a patient takes, and to reduce the
possibility of medicine resistance developing. The number of FDC
tablets is determined by a weight range of each patient at the start of
treatment
