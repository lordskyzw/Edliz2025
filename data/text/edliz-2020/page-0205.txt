General notes:

e In all pulmonary tuberculosis cases, sputum smear
examination is done at the end of two months, five months
and at the end of treatment. Refer to the National
Tuberculosis and Leprosy Management Guidelines for the
management of a positive smear at any stage in the
monitoring of treatment.

e Children weighing less than 25kg receive paediatric FDC
HRZ plus additional isoniazid and ethambutol.

e Children weighing 25kg and above receive adult formulations
and additional isoniazid.

e The total duration of treatment is 6 months (exceptions noted
in this chapter).

e Children with tuberculous meningitis or pericarditis,
disseminated or spinal disease with neurological
complications should be given 10HR (continuous phase)
ie. 10 months of isoniazid and rifampicin under direct
observation.

e Adults with TB of meninges, bone, joint, pericardium,
disseminated, or spinal disease should be given 6 HR
(continuous phase) i.e. 6 months of isoniazid and
rifampicin under direct observation.

FIXED DOSE COMBINATION OF ANTI-TB MEDICINES
The essential anti-TB medicines are available as FDCs such that
each tablet has a combination of 2 (2-FDC), 3 (3-FDC), or 4 (4-FDC)
medicines. The FDCs available in Zimbabwe are:

e RHZE: Rifampicin, lsoniazid, Pyrazinamide and Ethambutol

e RHdZ: Rifampicin, lsoniazid and Pyrazinamide

e RH: Rifampicin and Isoniazid
The number of FDC tablets is determined by a weight range of each
patient at the start of treatment as shown in table 10.7 and table 10.9.
