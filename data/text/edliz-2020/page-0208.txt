Drug Resistant Tuberculosis
Drug resistant TB (DR-TB) is the presence of bacilli resistant to one
or more anti-tuberculosis medicines. Resistance patterns are defined
as follows:
Mono-resistance Resistance to one first line drug only (H,
R, Z, EorS)
Poly-resistance Resistance to more than one first line
drug, excluding combination of H and R
