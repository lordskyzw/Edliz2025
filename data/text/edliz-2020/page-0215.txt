GENERAL NOTES
Zimbabwe is annually instituting Mass Drug (Medicine) Administration
(MDA) for bilharzia, intestinal worms, lymphatic filariasis and blinding
trachoma to Pre and School Aged Children (Pre-SAC and SAC) as well as
adults in endemic districts using WHO guidelines for preventive
chemotherapy.
