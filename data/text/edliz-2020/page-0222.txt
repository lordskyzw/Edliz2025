Erythema Nodosum Leprosum (ENL) Type Il reaction
In this reaction immune complex formation and deposition occurs with the
activation of complement. This type of reaction is characterised by crops of
tender subcutaneous nodules on the face, trunk and extensor surfaces of
the limbs. It may include systemic features such as fever, lymphadenitis,
orchitis, arthritis, nephritis, iridocyclitis and peripheral neuritis. Severe ENL
may also present with ulcerating and pustular lesions. The full dose of anti-
leprosy medicines should be continued in addition to the treatment of the
reaction.
