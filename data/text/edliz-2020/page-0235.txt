or Clindamycin), it should be given for a total of seven days.

SEVERE MALARIA

This is a life-threatening condition, and the goal of management is to prevent

death. Therapy should be initiated without delay.

Check for signs of:

= prostration, i.e. if the patient is unable to stand or sit or feed independently,
(children will be unable to breastfeed)

"persistent vomiting

=the slightest sign of alteration in consciousness which may indicate cerebral
malaria (refer to the Coma Scale).

Complications include any of the following:

= Cerebral malaria

« Bleeding tendencies

= Severe anaemia (Hb< or=6g/dl); (Hb < 7.5g/dl for non-immune patients)

= Hyperpyrexia

» Jaundice

= Shock

= Severe haemoglobinuria

= Hyperparasitaemia (>5% in non-immune patients)

= Acute renal failure

= Respiratory distress

= Hypoglycaemia
