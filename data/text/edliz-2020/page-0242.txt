TREATMENT OF SEVERE MALARIA AT COMMUNITY
LEVEL
When a patient presents with signs and symptoms of severe malaria
as a referral from the community health worker, he/she may have
received rectal artesunate if they were unable to take any medication
orally and time to get to the referral centre is more than 6 hours.
