In-patient management
Consider admission if patient is obviously unwell, or in severe pain.
Admission and close monitoring are mandatory if any of these signs are
present:
» respiratory distress
» cyanosis
» pulse >124/min
» — hypotension (systolic pressure < 90mmHg)
» temperature > 40°C or < 35°C
» altered mental state
. if elderly >65 years
» if patient has chronic lung disease (e.g. chronic obstructive
pulmonary disease), chronic renal failure, chronic cardiac
failure, chronic liver disease
