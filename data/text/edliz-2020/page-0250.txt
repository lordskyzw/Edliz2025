» All patients with a clinical diagnosis of COPD should have a
spirometry lung function testing. This is done to assess for
obstruction, assess the severity of the disease and to exclude asthma
by demonstrating reversibility or non-reversibility of the obstruction.

» Stop smoking and/or remove from hazardous (dusty) environment.

Treatment of COPD exacerbation

chest Xray infiltrates.
