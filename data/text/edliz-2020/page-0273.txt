Heart failure should be managed in the usual way.
All patients with carditis should be kept on strict bed rest until all evidence
of active carditis has resolved and the ESR has returned to normal. Activity
can then be gradually increased.
Antibiotic prophylaxis after rheumatic fever:
= Prophylaxis should be given to all patients with a history of rheumatic
fever and to those with heart valve lesions thought to be of rheumatic
origin. The optimum duration of prophylaxis is controversial but should
be continued up to at least 21 years of age. If at that age there are any
significant heart murmurs, prophylaxis should be life-long.
= Specific situations requiring prophylaxis for longer periods (can be
lifelong):
« definite carditis in previous attacks
« high risk of exposure to streptococcal infection at home or work
(crowded conditions, high exposure to children)
