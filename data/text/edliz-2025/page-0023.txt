MAJOR HIGHLIGHTS IN THE LATEST
Preamble
The major changes in this latest edition of EDLIZ will be highlighted here so that
you are aware of recommendations that you need to consider in your medicine
management or supply issues. Ideally each hospital should create its own local
medicine formulary which shows priority medicines in that setting to inform
forecasting, quantification and procurement. For instance, you should not keep
Specialist medicines if there is no Specialist to prescribe them. Hospital Medicine
and Therapeutics Committees should select medicines from the EDLIZ for use in
their hospital.

Sodium glucose co-transporter-2 (SGLT2) inhibitors
New medicines such as SGLT inhibitors, DPP4 (vildagliptin) have been included.
Vildagliptin is not yet in the WHO Essential medicines list and therefore the
medicines is under specialist’s care as we gather more evidence.
The sodium glucose co-transporter-2 (SGLT2) inhibitors are being introduced for
use via a Specialist i.e. will be an S medicine. They are used in Type 2 diabetes
mellitus to lower blood glucose levels as well as contributing to weight loss. They
have been shown to improve morbidity and mortality in heart failure as well as
retard the progression of chronic kidney disease.
