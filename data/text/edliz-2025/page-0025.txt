Management of Renal Tract Conditions (Adults and Children)
This chapter has been enhanced with recommendations specifically for children.
The first line treatment of UTI in adults remain as nitrofurantoin taken as twice a
day formulation.
Immunisation
There may be new vaccination schedules being added to our well-established
immunisation activities. These will be communicated through the EPI
programmes.
Human Papilloma Virus (HPV) vaccination has now become routinely available
for girls at 10 and 11 years old. Typhoid Conjugate Vaccine (TCV) was approved
for introduction into routine immunization in 2020.
Look-out for the introduction of “birth dose” of Hepatitis B Vaccine to be given as
soon after birth as possible.
There are discussions on introduction of routine Td (tetanus/diphtheria) booster
and further information will be provided in due course.
Sexually transmitted illnesses
Read the new recommendations for the treatment of gonorrhoea. In the
management of Neisseria gonorrhoea, the dose of ceftriaxone has been increased
to 1 gram as opposed to the previously used 250mg dose.
ART Guidelines
Always familiarize yourself with the latest HIV prevention, care _and treatment
guidelines ( ART guidelines).
Be on the lookout for circulars that MOHCC may release from time to time. Also
note that the ART guidelines are revised more frequently (usually every 2 years)
after the release of the World Health Organisation Guidelines.
