Fluid Requirements

e Infants > 1500g and > 32 weeks start on intermittent bolus feeds at 60mls/kg/day
and increased by 30mls/kg/day as tolerated to a maximum of 150mls/kg/day for
term babies and 200mls/kg/day for preterm infants.

e Infants < 1500g or < 32 weeks should receive IV fluids to allow gradual increase
of enteral feeds. Start enteral feeds at 20mls/kg/day and increase by 30
mls/kg/day.

e Keep infants <1500g/<32wks nil by mouth immediately after birth (unless
mother’s colostrum is present) to allow mum to start expressing breastmilk.
