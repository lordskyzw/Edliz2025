Necrotising enterocolitis
Give nothing by mouth. Supportive care is vital: oxygen, intravenous fluids, warmth,
and nasogastric continuous drainage. Anticipate complications such as bleeding,
vomiting, perforation, seizures. Refer for specialist diagnosis and care.
