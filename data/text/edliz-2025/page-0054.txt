Jaundice

Refer all babies developing jaundice within 24 hours of birth to a unit capable of

performing exchange transfusion.

Refer jaundiced babies who look ill.

e Jaundice developing in well babies may be treated using phototherapy. If
phototherapy equipment is not available, expose to the sun intermittently for a
maximum of two hours (keep warm). Shade the baby’s eyes with a loose-fitting
bandage over cotton wool pads. Continue until the baby is no longer yellow.

e Give an extra 20ml/kg/24 hrs of fluid. Be very careful that the baby does not get
cold (or hot). Encourage increased breastfeeding.
