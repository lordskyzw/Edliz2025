Chronic ear infection
Pus is seen draining from the ear and discharge is reported for 14 days or more.
Dry the ear by wicking
e Instil quinolone drops (such as ciprofloxacin, norfloxacin, or ofloxacin)
e Follow-up after 5 days then reassess.
e If not improving, refer to ENT specialist.
Managing a Child with a Sore Throat
Antibiotics are only needed for streptococcal sore throats to prevent complications such
as rheumatic fever. A streptococcal sore throat presents as tender enlarged lymph nodes
in front of the neck and a white exudate on the tonsils.
Sore throat but no swollen tender glands in neck and no pus on tonsils
¢ No antibiotics.
e Give paracetamol for pain.
e Feed child normally, continue breastfeeding.
Give plenty of fluids.
