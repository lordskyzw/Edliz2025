Cholera:

CASE DEFINITION: rice watery diarrhoea, with or without vomiting, causing

severe dehydration or death

In suspected cases immediately notify the Provincial Medical Director and obtain

current cholera guidelines. See also the chapter on gastrointestinal conditions.

e Rehydration is most important. The mainstay of cholera management is
rehydration, intravenously or orally.

e The use of antibiotics is strictly limited to very few indications such as: (i)
severe dehydration (ii) high attack rate within a household or congregate settings
(iii) as prophylaxis in areas described under (ii) or (iv) if specific guidance is
provided as part of the cholera response. Start antibiotics after the patient is
rehydrated and vomiting has stopped — usually after 4-6hrs.
