GENERAL NOTES
The terms Immunisation and Vaccination will be used interchangeably in this
chapter. Further information on immunisation may be found in the Manual for the
Zimbabwe Expanded Programme on Immunisation (ZEPI). Information relating
to rabies can be found in the chapter on tropical diseases. More vaccines have
been introduced since the last version of EDLIZ and additional vaccines are
expected to be introduced before next review. Vaccines currently under
consideration are Hepatitis B birth dose, Malaria and Hexavalent. Vaccines used
in response to outbreaks such as COVID-19, Oral Cholera, novel Oral Polio type
2 (nOPV2) are not discussed in this section.
