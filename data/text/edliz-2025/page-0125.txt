General principles
e@ medicines should be prescribed during pregnancy and lactation only if the
expected benefit to the mother outweighs the risk to the foetus or neonate;
e all medicines should be avoided if possible during the first trimester;
e well known medicines, which have been extensively used during pregnancy or
lactation, should be used in preference to new medicines;
