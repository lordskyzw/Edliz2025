CAUTION IN PREGNANCY: See chapter Obstetric and Gynaecological Conditions.

Doxycycline should not be used during pregnancy, or in lactating women. In pregnant

women chlamydial infection is best treated with azithromycin or erythromycin (which-

ever is available) while ceftriaxone should be used for gonococcal infection.
