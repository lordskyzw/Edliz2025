GRANULATING ULCERS WITHOUT BUBOES
These are most likely to be lesions of granuloma inguinale, a condition also known as
Donovanosis and caused by Calymmatobacterium granulomatis. It should be
remembered that persons who are immunosuppressed may not develop a bubo and
occasionally persistent genital ulcers without bubo formation may occur because of
chancroid in persons with immunosuppression and HIV infection.
