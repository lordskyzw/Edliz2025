ACUTE EPIDIDYMO-ORCHITIS
Acute scrotal swelling may occur in persons with acute epididymo-orchitis,
testicular torsion and scrotal trauma, and in those with irreducible or strangulated
inguinal hernia. Patients should be examined carefully in order to exclude these
conditions. If patients do not respond to first line therapy, they should be referred
for further investigations.
