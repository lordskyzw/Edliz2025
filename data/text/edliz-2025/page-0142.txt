GENITAL WARTS (CONDYLOMATA ACUMINATA)

e@ Podophyllin is toxic and should be used strictly under medical supervision.
Reported complications of Podophyllin include severe necrosis, scarring and
fistula-in-ano. Purified Podophyllotoxin causes less side effects and may be
more effective than Podophyllin. Podophyllotoxin can be self-administered to
ano-genital lesions as well as be applied at the local clinic. As more and more
people are tending to treat themselves with podophyllin, with adverse
consequences, health education needs to be given to ensure that pharmacists,
doctors and nurses cease providing Podophyllin for self-application by the
patient. If purified Podophyllotoxin is available, it can be given for self-
application by patients.

e Cervical, urethral, rectal and vaginal warts:

Do not use podophyllin. Treat with cryotherapy, electro-cautery, or by surgical
excision.
If the lesions are very extensive and large then the patient should be offered HIV
testing and counselling, referred to the OI Clinic or for specialist attention.
All patients with any STI should be referred for HIV testing.
