Immunization
A timely birth dose followed by three additional doses is the intervention that
leads to the greatest impact at the lowest cost.
e All infants should receive their first dose of hepatitis B vaccine as soon as
possible after birth, preferably within 24 hours.
e The birth dose should be followed by three additional doses at 6, 10 and 14
weeks to complete the Hepatitis B primary immunization series.
