If allergic to cotrimoxazole, try desensitization
Desensitization can be offered rapidly or over a longer period of time. Do not
desensitize anyone who has had an anaphylactic reaction to cotrimoxazole or a severe
skin rash such as Stevens-Johnson syndrome. Desensitization should be carried out
under clinical specialist supervision.
