Exclusion criteria for TPT
Adults and adolescents living with HIV should be screened with a clinical
algorithm; those who do not report any one of the symptoms of current cough,
fever, weight loss or night sweats are unlikely to have active TB and should be
offered TPT.
The following patients should be excluded:
e Patients who have symptoms and signs suggestive of active TB
Patients on treatment for TB
e@ PLHIV who have received TPT before and are not contacts of active TB cases
TB preventive treatment options
The following options are recommended for the treatment of latent TB infection
regardless of HIV status:
e Six months of daily isoniazid
e Three months of weekly rifapentine plus isoniazid
e Three months of daily isoniazid plus rifampicin
e Six months of daily levofloxacin
