PLHIV should take TPT as routine prevention at least once, preferably at the time of

HIV diagnosis and ART initiation. Repeat TPT for PLHIV is only recommended if they

become a contact of a bacteriologically- confirmed TB patient.

@ The recommended dose of INH (6H) in adults and adolescents is 5mg/kg/day to a
maximum of 300mg/day.

maximum dosage not supposed to exceed 300 mg). Refer to the table below for
