HIV RELATED WASTING SYNDROME (SLIM DISEASE)
DEFINITION: Weight loss of more than 10%, plus either unexplained chronic
diarrhoea for more than one month, or unexplained prolonged fever for more
than one month.
e It is important to exclude treatable conditions, especially TB, and to treat them
appropriately. A chest Xray is mandatory and a Urine LAM is recommended.
e Emaciation: encourage a high calorie and protein diet. Add mineral and vitamin
supplementation:
