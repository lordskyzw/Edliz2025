MANAGING ADVANCED HIV DISEASE
Advanced HIV Disease (AHD) is defined as WHO clinical stage 3 or 4 or a CD4
cell count equal or less than 200 cells/mm3 for people living with HIV aged five
years or older. All children younger than five years are considered to have
advanced HIV disease. This is based on the rationale that most children younger
than five years usually present for care with advanced immunosuppression,
younger children have an increased risk of disease progression and mortality
regardless of clinical and immune condition.
Assessing for AHD
CD4 cell count is the best indicator of disease stage and immediate risk of death
and thus should be used to identify people with advanced HIV disease. If access to
CD4 count is limited or unavailable, WHO staging should be used.
