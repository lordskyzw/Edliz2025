Recommended treatment regimens for adolescents and adults
The choice of medicine regimen is based on the “essential medicine” concept and
the rational use of medicine. To maximise adherence, use of fixed-dose
combination (FDC) medicines is strongly encouraged.
The choice of ARVs has been based on evidence of efficacy and safety, on
availability and cost of medications, as well as on the side effects profile and the
potential for development of resistance. The national ART programme uses the
following FDCs in the first line regimens:
