EDLIZ 2025

Zimbabwe is implementing universal triple prophylaxis for ALL HIV-exposed

infants, rather than adopting the full risk-stratification model. It is recommended

that:

e Administer AZT + 3TC + NVP for 6 weeks for all HIV-exposed infants
(continuing triple therapy with established drugs).

e@ Step-down: NVP monotherapy from 6 weeks until end of breastfeeding or
until maternal viral suppression is confirmed.

e ANAT should be performed prior to initiating the triple regimen. If the result is
not immediately available, at least collect a specimen, as combination ARV
prophylaxis may reduce the sensitivity of diagnostic testing for approximately
2-6 weeks after discontinuation.
