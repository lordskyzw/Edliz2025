Monitoring children on ART

e Growth should be monitored and development assessed.

e Infant feeding practices should be reviewed regularly and appropriate supportive
counselling and appropriate referrals provided.

e Check haemoglobin after at least 6-8 weeks if on Zidovudine

e Use urine dipsticks to check for glycosuria and estimated glomerular filtration
rate (eGFR) and/or serum creatinine when on Tenofovir

e Check CD4 count at ART initiation (in children older than 5 years), for clients
returning to care and those with suspected or confirmed treatment failure.

e Conduct viral load testing 6 months after ART initiation, 12 months after ART
initiation and once every year thereafter or when clinical signs are suggestive of
treatment failure

e Opportunistic infections should be evaluated at every visit.

RECOMMENDED SECOND-LINE TREATMENT FOR CHILDREN

Definition of treatment failure in children

Clinical Failure:

New or recurrent clinical event indicating advanced or severe immunodeficiency

(WHO clinical stage 3 and 4, clinical condition with exception of TB) after 6

months of effective treatment

Immunological failure:

In children younger than 5 years: CD4 percentage <15%

Children older than 5 years - CD4 levels persistently below 200 cells/mm?

Virological failure:

Plasma viral load above 1000 copies/ ml based on two consecutive viral load

measurements after 3-months, with adherence support.
