PRE-EXPOSURE PROPHYLAXIS (PREP)
Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) is the use of ARVs by people who are HIV
negative before HIV exposure to prevent the acquisition of HIV. With optimum
adherence, oral PrEP is more than 90% effective in reducing the risk of HIV
infection.
Eligibility criteria for oral PrEP
Oral PrEP should be offered to individuals who are HIV negative and are at
substantial risk of HIV infection. An HIV test must be done before PrEP is
initiated. Individuals at high risk have:
e Multiple sexual partners
e Inconsistent and incorrect use of condoms
e Female sex workers (FSWs) and those who engage in transactional sex
e Men who have sex with men (MSM)
e Use or abuse of injectable drugs and alcohol
e Episode(s) of STI within the last six months
e Discordant couples, especially if the HIV positive partner is not on ART or has
been on ART for less than six months or those with high viral load
e Recurrent users of PEP
In addition, individuals should commit and be supported to adhere to PrEP. Sub-
populations considered to be at high risk for HIV infection include people in sero-
discordant relationships, sex workers, and long-distance truck drivers, men who
have sex with other men (MSM), adolescent girls and young women and pregnant
and breastfeeding women.
