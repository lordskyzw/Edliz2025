TUBERCULOSIS CASE MANAGEMENT
Diagnosis of TB
The presence of pulmonary tuberculosis should be suspected in individuals
presenting with one or more of the following complaints:
e Cough for 1 week or longer, or a cough of any duration among PLHIV
e Night sweats,
e Fever,
e Loss of weight,
ea BMI <17kg/m’ or failure to thrive among children (z score less than 1).
INVESTIGATIONS
Chest X-Rays
Chest X-ray (with or without artificial intelligence [AI]) is recommended as a
priority SCREENING test for persons classified as being at high risk for
tuberculosis. The CAD AI score threshold to collect sputum depends on the
software and version and should be obtained from the National TB Programme.
Abnormal CXR by CASD score or by human reader should prompt the collection
of sputum. The following persons are classified as being at high risk for
tuberculosis:
e Children under the age of 5 years.
e People living with HIV.
@ Those who are malnourished.
e People above 60 years of age.
e People with diabetes mellitus.
e People who drink alcohol excessively.
@ Miners and ex-miners.
e People in congregate settings.
e Inmates and correctional facility communities.
e Health care workers.
Note: In the presence of clinical improvement, it is not necessary to monitor the
response of pulmonary TB to treatment by chest x-rays.
