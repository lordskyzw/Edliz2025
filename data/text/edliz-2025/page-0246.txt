Any patient showing a positive skin smear should be treated with the MDT
regimen for multibacillary (MB) leprosy, irrespective of the clinical classification.
When classification is in doubt, the patient should be treated as MB leprosy.
Treatment of Paucibacillary Patients
Screening of family contacts should be performed.
Since 2017, a 3-drug regimen should be used for all leprosy patients with
rifampicin, dapsone and clofazimine.
