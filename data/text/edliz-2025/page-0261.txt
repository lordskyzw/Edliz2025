TREATMENT FAILURE

Treatment failure, should be suspected clinically if there is no response after

48 hours of correct therapy, and a change to second line therapy made

immediately.

| Early treatment failure is formally diagnosed if a patient

e Is still febrile 72hrs after initial therapy and has parasitaemia on day 3 with
axillary temperature >/= 37.5

e Worsening of clinical conditions anytime during day1l-day3 in the presence
of parasitaemia.

Late treatment failure is diagnosed when

e There is development of danger signs or severe malaria after day 3 in the
presence of parasitaemia

e Incomplete resolution/recurrence of parasitaemia and fever: presence of
parasitaemia and axillary temperature >/=37.S(or history of fever) on any
day from day 4 to day 28
(please refer to WHO treatment guidelines for more details)

Treatment failure may be due to:

e Inadequate therapy, e.g. medicine being vomited within 30 mins under
dosing or failure to complete the treatment.

e Wrong classification of patients severe malaria as uncomplicated malaria.

e Malaria parasite resistance (known or suspected) to the given medicine.

Ifa patient returns to the health facility still feeling unwell:

e Check for other conditions e.g. meningitis, ARI, gastro-enteritis.

e Check for signs of severe and complicated malaria
