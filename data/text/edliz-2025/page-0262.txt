SECOND LINE TREATMENT OF UNCOMPLICATED MALARIA IN
ADULTS UNABLE TO TOLERATE ARTESUNATE-AMODIAQUINE IS
ORAL QUININE WITH DOXYCYCLINE OR CLINDAMYCIN
Each Quinine tablet contains quinine sulphate 300mg
Treatment schedule for second line therapy: Adults (who cannot tolerate
ASAQ)
Medicine Codes Dose Frequency Duration
quinine po Cc Vv 600mg every 8hours 7 days

N.B:-
1. *Doxycycline is contraindicated in children below 8 years and in pregnancy
and these patients should complete the 7-day quinine course.
2. Clindamycin is used in place of Doxycycline in pregnancy during the
first trimester and children under the age of eight years. (see Treatment in
Special Groups: Uncomplicated Malaria in Pregnancy)
