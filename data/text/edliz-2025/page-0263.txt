WHO recommends a low dose primaquine to minimise the chances of

clinically significant haemolysis where G6PD assay has not been done.

The following patients should be given Primaquine 0.25 mg base/kg body

weight with first dose of ACT for uncomplicated malaria:

e Above one year old

e Body weight above 10kg

e Not pregnant

Primaquine comes as 26.3 mg Primaquine phosphate equivalent to 15 mg and

Primaquine base or 13.2 mg Primaquine phosphate equivalent to 7.5 mg

Primaquine base tablets. The dosage tables below use the 15mg tablet as

reference. Titrate the dosage based on the available strength of product.

Dilution instruction for primaquine tablet:

@ Mix 15 ml water with one crushed tablet of 15 mg Primaquine base, making

30kg, crush two tablets of the 7.5mg tablet in 15ml of water. This makes a
