In-patient management
Consider admission if patient is obviously unwell, or in severe pain. Admission
and close monitoring are mandatory if any of these signs are present:
e respiratory distress
@ cyanosis
e pulse >124/min
e hypotension (systolic pressure < 90mmHg)
e temperature > 40°C or < 35°C
e altered mental state
e ifelderly >65 years
e if patient has chronic lung disease (e.g. chronic obstructive pulmonary disease),
chronic renal failure, chronic cardiac failure, chronic liver disease
e Scoring for pneumonia severity (CURB-65) (the presence of any of the
