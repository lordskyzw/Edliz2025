OTHER COMMON RESPIRATORY INFECTIONS

Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)

This term has replaced “chronic bronchitis and emphysema”.

There are many aspects of management:

e All patients with a clinical diagnosis of COPD should have a spirometry lung
function testing. This is done to assess for obstruction, assess the severity of the
disease and to exclude asthma by demonstrating reversibility or non-
reversibility of the obstruction.

e Stop smoking and/or remove from hazardous (dusty) environment.

e@ Prompt treatment of infective exacerbations (or as for pneumonia):

Treatment of COPD exacerbation

Give antibiotics if sputum colour has changed to purulent, or there is fever or new

chest Xray infiltrates.
