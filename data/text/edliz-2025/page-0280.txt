Bronchiectasis (Non cystic fibrosis)

The hallmark of treatment are:

e Prompt treatment of infective exacerbations with broad spectrum antibiotics.
Exacerbations are characterized by an increase in volume of sputum, change in
sputum colour from white to yellowish or green plus a fever.

e Referral to physiotherapist for postural drainage and physiotherapy.

e Always send sputum to the laboratory for microscopy, culture and sensitivity
together with ZN stain/ Gene expert testing.

e To prevent exacerbations, patients should get an annual flu vaccine and a five
yearly pneumococcal vaccine.

e Frank haemoptysis warrants referral to a Specialist.

Acute Exacerbations of Bronchiectasis

Aetiology includes S. pneumonia, H. influenza, P. aeruginosa, Moraxella,

Mycobacteria and sometimes fungi.

e Antibiotics should be chosen to empirically cover suspected pathogens. Adjust
treatment when microbiology results are available.

@ Inhaled bronchodilators

e Persistent haemoptysis requires cardiothoracic surgeon’s attention.
