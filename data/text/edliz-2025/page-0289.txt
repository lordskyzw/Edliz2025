MODERATE ASTHMA - CHILDREN
Wheezing episodes more frequent >/= 3 per year. These may be triggered by
infections, allergies, exercise etc. Treatment is as for mild asthma, but daily low-
dose inhaled corticosteroid (ICS) is required as a controller/preventer. Family
history of asthma, atopy (allergic rhinitis, atopic dermatitis) and eosinophilia >/=
4% suggest asthma. Exclude differential diagnosis.
