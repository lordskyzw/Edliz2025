* Thrombolytic agents should be administered early preferably in infarcts of less
than 12 hours duration.
* CAUTIONS: DO not give digoxin in acute infarction unless there is a supra-
ventricular arrhythmia that requires it.
* DO not use inotropic agents such as isoprenaline or adrenaline as they may be
counter-productive and cause an extension of the infarction.
