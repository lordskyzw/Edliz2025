ATRIAL FIBRILLATION

Patients with acute, symptomatic atrial fibrillation should be referred to the nearest

hospital for stabilization.

Patients with atrial fibrillation confirmed by ECG, should be assessed for burden

of symptoms, cardiac function and stroke (or systemic embolism) risk, using

validated scores such as the CHA2DS2Vasc score.

The following medications can be used for rate control:
