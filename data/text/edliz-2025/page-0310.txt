Cardiovascular Disease in Children
Hypertension in Children
The causes of hypertension in children are mostly secondary, the majority of which
are related to renal disease. On initial assessment, a high BP should prompt further
investigation of underlying conditions. First line treatment will depend on the
known or suspected cause of the hypertension.
Cardiac failure:
Heart failure is a state in which the cardiac output is inadequate to meet metabolic
demands.
General notes
Causes of heart failure in children are variable and include some congenital heart
defects and acquired heart diseases such as valvular heart diseases and
cardiomyopathies.
Clinical presentation of heart failure in children may vary based on the child’s age.
The following features may suggest heart failure in children:
e History: fast breathing and or excessive sweating especially during feeds,
failure to thrive, easy fatigability or inability to keep up with peers.
