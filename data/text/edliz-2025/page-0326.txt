Malabsorption Syndromes
Correction of electrolyte and nutritional deficiencies is important. Individual
conditions require specific treatment: lactase deficiency, pancreatic insufficiency
and pernicious anaemia have specific management. Specialist referral
recommended.
Chronic Pancreatitis
e Exclude alcohol, gallstones, hypercalcaemia, and lipid disorders.
e Pain control must be tailored to each patient and opiates may be required.
e Treat diabetes as necessary. Give enzyme supplements and acid suppression for
malabsorption.
@ Cessation of alcohol intake is imperative.
e Referral to a specialist is recommended.
