Acute Liver Failure/ Hepatic Encephalopathy
e Identify and eliminate precipitating causes (electrolyte derangements, toxins,
septicaemia, alcohol, upper GI bleeding).
e Stop all unnecessary medicines including diuretics and sedatives.
e Intensive support, including fluid management, assessment for infection and
metabolic parameters and detect bleeding.
@ Give high calorie diet (2000 kcal/day), and low protein diet.
e Look for aetiology (acute hepatitis B, medications, herbs and alcohol commonly)
