General measures in the management of acute kidney injury

e Catheter: Insert a urinary catheter but remove it once sustained diuresis has
occurred or if oliguria persists and the patient is started on dialysis or
conservative treatment. Avoid long catheterisation periods - increases risk of
catheter related infection.

e Urine: Ward urinalysis test - check for haematuria, proteinuria and glycosuria.
Send urine for microscopy (for cells, casts), culture and sensitivity.

e Diet: High calorie and normal or high protein diet with low sodium and low
potassium. Ask for dietician’s help. For low potassium diet, advise to avoid e.g.
oranges, bananas, mangoes, potatoes.

e Anaemia: If oliguric do not transfuse unless there is significant bleeding, as
there is a high risk of fluid overload. Can transfuse safely when patient is on
dialysis.

e Monitor BP regularly( e.g. 4 hourly)

e Monitor fluid input/output strictly.

e Avoid nephrotoxic medicines such as gentamicin and NSAIDs

e Watch for and treat underlying infections.

e Check weight daily.
