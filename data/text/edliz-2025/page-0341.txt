Urinary tract infections
Recommend urgent urine microscopy and culture, and the immediate
commencement of antibiotics ift
1. Age <3 months
2. Specific urinary symptoms in children of 3 months to 2 years of age
3.Non-specific symptoms in children of 3 months to 2 years of age at high risk of
serious illness.

In children 3 years of age and older a urine dipstick should be used to initially
diagnose UTI.
Factors distinguishing upper UTI and lower UTI
Bacteriuria and fever of = 38 degrees Upper UTI
Bacteriuria, loin pain/tenderness and fever of < 38 degrees Upper UTI
Bacteriuria, but no systemic features Lower UTI
