Refer all cases of chronic polyarthritis for a definitive diagnosis. This group shares

Rheumatoid Arthritis and Juvenile Chronic Arthritis (Juvenile
Rheumatoid Arthritis)
To avert the erosive damage of progressive rheumatoid arthritis, early diagnosis
and initiation of treatment with NSAIDs, Disease Modifying Anti-Rheumatic
Medicines (DMARDs) (hydroxychloroquine or chloroquine, methotrexate and
sulphasalazine), and low dose steroids in the presence of severe inflammation or
vasculitis is necessary. Disease modifying medicines and biologics are the
mainstay of treatment to minimise erosions and deformities. Biologics should
only be prescribed by Specialists as there will be need for close monitoring of the
patient. Screen for TB, HIV, hepatitis B and C prior to starting DMARDs.
