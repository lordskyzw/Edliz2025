GENERAL ADVICE FOR DIABETICS

All diabetic patients should have a "medic-alert" bracelet or necklace

Syringes / Insulin Storage:

e Store syringes dry.

e Do not sterilize insulin syringes

© use insulin syringe only once to guarantee sterility

e Insulin should be stored in a cool place.

Injection technique

e Clean and dry skin. Inject subcutaneously at right angles 90 degrees to the skin
counting up to 6-10 seconds before removing the needle.

e The site of injection should be varied (abdomen and thighs, shoulders, gluteal
region and back of arm are the most suitable sites).

Foot Care for Diabetics:

e Advice about foot care is important: keep feet clean and dry, wear well-fitting
shoes, and take care to avoid blisters and burns. Healthcare service provider to
screen for diabetic foot at each review visit.

Ophthalmological Examinations:

e At least annually from the time of diagnosis; monitor and record acuities (each
eye separately). If acuity drops, look for cataracts. Refer to an eye
hospital/specialist.

e Pregnant women should be screened for retinopathy in each trimester and
preconception because retinopathy can get worse in pregnancy.
