Blood pressure control:

e@ Good BP control is essential and is more effective at preventing complications
than good glycaemic control. Use combinations of medicines, preferably
including an ACEI, target BP <140/80. If Chronic Kidney Disease (eGFR <
60ml/hr or proteinuria) target BP < 120/80.

Aspirin and diabetes

e Use for secondary prevention (coronary artery disease, stroke, peripheral
vascular disease, high cardiovascular risk, chronic kidney disease, peripheral
neuropathy)

Lipid control

All type 2 diabetes should be on a statin e.g. Atorvastatin 20mg oral daily for the

management of hyperlipidaemia.

Type 1 diabetics with complications or above 40 years should be started on a statin.

Smoking:

e Patients with diabetes should avoid smoking.

Sexual Dysfunction:

e Patients (males and females) MUST always be asked about sexual dysfunction
and referred accordingly, since it is a marker of vascular disease.

e They can be offered sildenafil or tadalafil tablets after evaluation for endocrine
causes that test for HbAlc, prolactin, TSH and fasting morning total
testosterone.

Medicine Codes Adult dose Frequency Duration
sildenafil po B E 25mg once a day when necessary
