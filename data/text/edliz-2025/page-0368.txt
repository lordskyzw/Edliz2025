Metabolic conditions in children
Hypoglycaemia in Children
Common causes of hypoglycaemia include excessive insulin dose, skipping meals,
and exercise. In children who are awake- give 100% juice, glucose tablet or a
sweet, to increase the glucose fast. Thereafter maintain glucose level with a
carbohydrate e.g. sandwich
