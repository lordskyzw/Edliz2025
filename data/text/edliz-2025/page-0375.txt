Further management
The combination of fever and focal neurological signs is an indication for referral
to a central hospital and CT scan of the head.
The differential diagnosis includes cerebral abscess, cryptococcal meningitis,
tuberculoma, toxoplasma encephalitis, and other parasitic infection.
