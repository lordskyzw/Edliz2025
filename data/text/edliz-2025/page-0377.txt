TREATMENT OF PRIMARY HEADACHE

Tension

e Bilateral; dull; band-like, worse as the day wears on; no nausea; frontal or
occipital in site; often daily; can continue activities.

e Social circumstances may precipitate these headaches; counselling in relaxation
therapy (muscle relaxation) will help. Lifestyle changes may help (lunchtime
rest, more sleep), and physiotherapy if there is local muscle spasm and
tenderness.
