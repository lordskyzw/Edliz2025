Second line treatment

For the patient with persistent generalised seizures despite sodium valproate check

the diagnosis, compliance, medicine interactions, and intercurrent illness.

e Increase dose of sodium valproate fortnightly up to maximum dose of 30mg/kg

Tertiary/Specialist care

Decisions will include whether further investigations (EEG, CT scan) are indicated,

and the use of phenytoin sodium, sodium valproate, ethosuximide, diazepam or

clonazepam.
