STATUS EPILEPTICUS
A seizure or a series of seizures continuing for more than 30 minutes, or recurrent
seizures without regaining consciousness in-between, for more than 30 minutes.
Many cases do not occur in known epileptic patients — always consider possible
underlying causes such as stroke or brain abscess.
The above description should be strictly adhered to. The practice of prescribing
diazepam 10mg i.v. every time a seizure occurs should be resisted. It is
preferable to use a regular anti-convulsant during the in-patient stay.
