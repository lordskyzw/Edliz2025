CAUTION - Benzodiazepines

e Do not administer at a rate over 5 mg/minute.

e Benzodiazepines, especially diazepam IV, can cause respiratory depression

e Monitor vital signs closely during and after administration of benzodiazepines.
In the frail and elderly patient or where respiratory depression is a concern,
reduce the dose by half.

e Switch to oral administration, once containment is achieved.

e The safest route of administration is oral followed by IM with the IV route
having the highest risk of respiratory depression and arrest. Use the safest route
wherever possible.

e Use low Risperidone (0.5- 3 mg/day) or haloperidol instead of benzodiazepines
in patients with respiratory insufficiency.

e In the short-term, benzodiazepines can aggravate delirium.

e Allow at least 15-30 minutes for the medication to take effect. Repeated IM
doses of benzodiazepines may result in toxicity owing to accumulation.

e Secure airway.

e Exclude hypoglycaemia.
