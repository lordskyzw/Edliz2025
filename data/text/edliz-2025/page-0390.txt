EDLIZ 2025
GENERAL MEASURES
Acute management
e Assess airway, breathing, circulation and disability.
e Measure blood glucose and treat hypoglycaemia if present
e BP is often elevated in acute stroke, see BP management recommendations
below
e Patients should be given nil by mouth until swallowing is formally assessed.
INVESTIGATIONS
All patients:
- RPR, TPHA, HIV, Fasting lipid profile, fasting plasma glucose/HBAIC, FBC,
U+E, CXR, ECG.
-CT brain (differentiation between haemorrhage and infarct cannot be predicted on
the clinical grounds)
Additional tests
e If Pregnant- antiphospholipid antibodies
e If HIV positive, consider LP to exclude meningitis, if not contraindicated.
Treatment
e Currently thrombolytic therapy is not easily accessible
e Acute treatment:

aspirin po Cc V_ 150mg once daily pre-referral
