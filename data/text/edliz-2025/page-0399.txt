Hypoadrenalism
In children, hypoadrenalism can be caused by Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia
(CAH). Patients are unable to produce cortisol and/or aldosterone. This usually
presents in childhood with non-specific signs. In females can present with
ambiguous genitalia. Children with ambiguous genitalia should be closely
monitored at birth for signs of salt wasting.
Children with dehydration, hyponatremia, hyperkalaemia should be considered to
be in Adrenal crisis and will require prompt treatment and referral.
