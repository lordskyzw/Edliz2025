Substance/medication-induced psychotic disorder and psychotic
disorder due to another medical condition
HIV/AIDS
e Psychosis in HIV/AIDS may be caused by HIV virus, ART and opportunistic
infections.
e Patients who are HIV infected are more susceptible to antipsychotic side effects,
therefore, use lower doses and observe for the side effects.
Other medical conditions causing psychosis
e Infections such as malaria, syphilis, tuberculosis and others.
e Traumatic Brain Injury and tumors.
e Systemic, Endocrine and Metabolic Conditions such as kidney diseases, thyroid
disease, diabetes mellitus, electrolyte imbalance and others.
e Withdrawals from substances such as alcohol may often present as a psychotic
disorder.
