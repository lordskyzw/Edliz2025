Side effects and adverse reactions of anti-psychotic medicines
@ Early side effects:

e@ Chlorpromazine and sulpiride may cause drowsiness, dizziness, postural
hypotension, dry mouth, blurred vision and galactorrhoea: usually in early
stages of treatment and may be self-limiting. These should be discussed
with patients and a change to a first line medicine considered if such side
effects are limiting compliance and adherence.

e Extra-pyramidal side effects which include acute dystonia [common features
are body stiffness, tongue protrusion, grimacing, writhing, twisting of neck
or body, torticollis, and oculogyric crisis], Parkinsonism and akathisia.
