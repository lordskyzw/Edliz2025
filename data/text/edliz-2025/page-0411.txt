MOOD (AFFECTIVE) DISORDERS
Bipolar Disorders
It is a condition characterised by elation (mania) and low mood (depression).
Treatment depends on presentation. If acutely disturbed use of antipsychotic
medication for rapid tranquilization is recommended. (as per section on
psychosis). Thereafter use antipsychotics as for section on antipsychotics but
avoid Sulpiride and Clozapine for this group of patients.
Treatment is as for other psychoses i.e. with antipsychotics but add mood
stabilisers.
