Autism spectrum disorders (ASDs)
Core features of autism include deficits in social communication and social
interaction such as delayed or absent speech, echolalia; repetitive stereotypical
behaviours such as repetitive motor movements, restricted repetitive or ritualistic
behaviours interests or activities and resistance to change. In addition, there may
be aggression, irritability and self-injurious behaviour
Management of ASDs
General principles
Assessment management and coordination of care should be done by specialists.
Interventions are aimed at improving communication and activities of daily living
and consist mainly of behavioural modification, social skills training, speech
therapy and educational interventions.
Medications in ASDs
Psychotropic medications such as risperidone and aripiprazole are sometimes used
to manage uncontrollable irritability, hyperactivity, self-mutilation and repetitive
motor stereotypes. Psychotropics should only be considered after behavioural and
environmental interventions have been inadequate or the child poses a safety risk.
