Conduct disorders (CD)
Conduct disorders are characterized by repetitive persistent patterns of antisocial
aggressive and defiant behaviour that results in significant and persistent violation
of age-appropriate social expectations and rules.
Management of CD
The mainstay management approaches are psychosocial and behavioural
interventions mostly focused on parent training, anger management, problem
solving techniques and primary prevention measures.
Pharmacological interventions are used mainly to target comorbid conditions
such as ADHD and epilepsy. Antipsychotics such as risperidone and aripiprazole
can be used to manage aggressive behaviour not responding to psychosocial and
behavioural approaches. Please refer to the dosing guide above.
Mood stabilizers such as carbamazepine can also be used for the management of
aggression in CD.
