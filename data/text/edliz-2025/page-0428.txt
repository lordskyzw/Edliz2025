HIV/AIDS and Mental Health

There is a complex interlink between HIV and Mental Health problems

e HIV can have a direct neurobiological effect on the brain and thus increases the
risk of certain mental health disorders (e.g. depression, anxiety, psychosis)

e HIV also has a psychological effect on patients as a chronic illness requiring
lifelong treatment, health anxiety, stigma, issues of guilt and self-blame can also
be prominent

e@ Some mental health conditions can also increase risky behaviours and increase
risk of contracting HIV (e.g. disinhibition in mania, intellectual disability)

e Some HIV medications may also affect mental health (e.g. Zidovudine,
Efavirenz)

e Mental health problems can affect adherence to HIV medications and
compromise HIV outcomes

Assessment for and management of mental health problems is integral to holistic

HIV care

e Pre and post Test Counselling is vital in providing psychosocial support from
the start of the treatment journey

e Discuss the reason for testing and assess risk behaviour and discuss risk
reducing interventions

e Discuss the meaning of and possible reactions to a positive result

e Address fears and anxiety

e Interpret the result clearly

e Discuss prevention of transmission and disclosure

e Explore available psychosocial support were indicated

e Screening for common mental health disorders using standardized tools should
be part of the routine assessment in HIV care clinics (e.g. PHQ 9, SSQ) with
appropriate referral were indicated
