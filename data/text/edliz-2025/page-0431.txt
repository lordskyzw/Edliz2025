Sleep Disorders
Sleeping medications can be addictive and if given, care should be taken on the
duration they are given. It is generally not recommended to rely on prescription
sleeping pills for more than 2 weeks, and other remedies should be used instead.
These include sleep hygiene, treatment of any underlying mental health issues, use
of other psychological services, and avoidance of alcohol and other drugs of abuse.
Examples of medications which can be used include:
e Melatonin C-DRUG
e Antihistamines C-DRUG
A psychiatrist should assess the patient and determine the doses of medication to
be given.
