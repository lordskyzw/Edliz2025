Important: Avoid the use of steroid eye preparations; conditions requiring them
need confirmation by a specialist - refer. Steroids may lead to worsening of
infective processes like trachoma, increased intraocular pressure, cataracts,
delayed healing and worsening of corneal ulcers of viral origin. Never use local
anaesthetic drops for painful corneal conditions. Only specialists should prescribe
atropine eye drops/ointment.
DIAGNOSIS OF EYE CONDITIONS
Testing vision is the single most important test, and every health worker must
know how to use an eyesight-testing chart e.g. Snellen Chart or LOGMAR Chart
for both adults and E-Chart or Landolt rings or pictorial charts for children and
illiterate patients. Visual acuity testing should be done in a well light room at
measured distance from the testing object. Use of pinhole can quickly give an idea
of patients whose vision is likely to improve with use of spectacles.
With a simple torch, most of the external eye diseases can be diagnosed. However,
the use of a slit lamp should be standard practice.
