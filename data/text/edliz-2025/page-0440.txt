Treatment of conjunctivitis:
e* Eyelids hygiene using baby shampoo or clean water is important as part of
management of these conditions.
e Acute bacterial conjunctivitis:
Medicine Codes Adult dose Frequency Duration

e@ Viral conjunctivitis:
No medicine treatment as this is a self-resolving infection. If in doubt, treat as for
acute bacteria and refer.
e Allergic conjunctivitis:
Educate/ reassure. Apply cold compresses and wear a sun hat whenever outdoors. If
no relief of symptoms refer. A night-time dose of an antihistamine may relieve
symptoms.
NB: Steroids are contraindicated in allergic conjunctivitis
