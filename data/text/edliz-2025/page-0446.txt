CATARACT
Cataract is defined as the opacity of the lens. Causes may be classified as acquired
and congenital. Aging, trauma, inflammation, drugs and metabolic disorders like
diabetes mellitus are the leading acquired causes while intrauterine infections,
inheritance and congenital abnormalities are the leading congenital causes of
cataracts. Treatment for cataract is surgical except for galactosemic cataract which
reverses when the neonate is given a lactose free diet. Cataracts present with a
white pupil in children, loss of vision of various degrees, squint and rarely painful
blind eye if neglected and complicated with glaucoma and lens induced uveitis.
