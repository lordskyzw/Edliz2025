Persistent Generalized Lymphadenopathy (PGL)
The commonest cause of generalized enlarged lymph nodes (> lcm) is underlying
HIV. Thus, the patient should be considered for PITC. Evaluate the patient for
possible lymphoma and/or loco-regional malignancies.
Oral Ulcers
These are painful ulcers that may occur anywhere in the oral mucosa. They may
cause pain or discomfort during oral feeds. Apart from herpes simplex, most are
treated symptomatically by using simple analgesics. Large ulcers may need biopsy
to exclude malignancy.
The following applied to the mouth area may help:
