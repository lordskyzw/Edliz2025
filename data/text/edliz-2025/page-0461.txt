Complicated Sinusitis
This occurs when infection spreads beyond the confines of the sino-nasal mucosa.
It presents with orbital and /or intracranial complications.
Orbital complications are characterised by preseptal cellulitis, orbital cellulitis,
subperiosteal abscess and orbital abscess.
