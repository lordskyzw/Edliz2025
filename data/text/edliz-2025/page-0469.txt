Erysipelas
A superficial cellulitis with lymphatic vessel involvement, due to streptococcal
infection.
Begins as a small break in the skin or umbilical stump (children). Area affected
has a growing area of redness and swelling, accompanied by high fever and pains.
Treat with:

Medicine Codes Adult dose Frequency Duration

erythromycin po C V_ 250-500mg 4 times a day 7 days

[Paed = 125-250mg]

benzathine penicillin C V_ 1.2g single dose
In penicillin allergy
Erysipelas tends to recur in the same area. If recurrent episode, increase duration
of antibiotic to 10-14 days.
Acute Cellulitis

Streptococci or Staphylococci.

sharply demarcated margins from uninvolved skin]. Give antibiotics:
