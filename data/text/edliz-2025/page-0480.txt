Introduction
Burns are a very common injury in this country. One of the leading causes of
admission in surgical wards, particularly in children. A very common cause of
morbidity and mortality if not handled correctly.
Causes:
1. Thermal (Hot and Cold)
2. Chemical
3. Radiation
4. Electrical
Classification Of Burns
These are classified according to
e Burn Surface Area (BSA)
e Depth Of Burn
Depth of Burn is further divided into:
e Superficial
e Superficial Partial Thickness
(Painful, inflamed and usually have blisters)
e Deep partial thickness
e Deep
(Painless in the centre of burn with inflammation and sensitivity at edges)
Assessment
Initial brief assessment with the ATLS principles of:
1. airway,
2. breathing and
3. circulation.
Assess Burnt Surface Area
Evaluation of Burnt Surface Area
