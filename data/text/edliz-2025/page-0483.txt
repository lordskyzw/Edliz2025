Burns caused by heat
There is need for immediate cooling by immersion in water at approximately 25°C
for 15mins to 30mins; then apply simple dry dressings (remove clothing if not
adherent to burn and wrap in a clean cloth).
Chemical Burns
If there is dry powder present, brush off the excess and then wash preferably with
large amounts of running water for at least 20 minutes. Seal with soft paraffin
(Vaseline) only what cannot be extracted with water. Treatment of chemical
exposure should begin with immediate removal of contaminated clothing, shoes,
socks and jewellery. Avoid contaminating skin that has not been in contact with
the chemical.
