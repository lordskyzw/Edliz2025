Physiotherapy
It is very important to prevent disability and disfigurement. Physiotherapy also
serves to prevent hypostatic pneumonia. Start physiotherapy early.
Special regions/problem burns
Circumferential burns} Can constrict when swelling develops. This is
of trunk, limbs or particularly a feature of deep burns.
digits
Eyes Saline irrigation plus tetracycline or chloramphenicol
eye ointment 4 hourly. Refer to eye hospital for
specialist care.
Apply soft paraffin (Vaseline) three times a day.
Face Apply burn cream daily; SSD not to be used on the face
as it causes damage to the eyes.
Keep neck extended and head up (i.e. nurse half-seated).
Hands / feet Elevate limbs. Dress with burn cream. Hands may be
nursed free in a plastic bag with burn cream*, changed
daily. Splint wrists.
Perineum Catheterise early using sterile preparation. Apply burn
cream* twice daily.
* burn cream is the term used to denote either silver sulphadiazine or
povidone iodine cream
