SEVERE PAIN

e Morphine is the medicine of choice. It should be given orally wherever
possible and only rectally or parenterally (subcutaneously, intramuscularly,
intravenously) in patients who cannot swallow.

e There are 2 commonly found oral morphine preparations in Zimbabwe i.e. short-
acting (4hourly) and long-acting (12hourly).

e In severed pain, codeine should be discontinued however, a mild analgesic given
with morphine may be useful (largely because of the anti-inflammatory effect of
WHO Pain Ladder level 1 analgesics).

e An anti-inflammatory — or other adjuvant analgesic - may be needed.

e Morphine (short-acting) is usually given 4 hourly, and a “breakthrough pain”
dose may be added at ANY time, the dose added being 60 — 100% of the current
4-hrly dose. Any need for more than 3 breakthrough doses in 24hours means
pain is not adequately controlled.
