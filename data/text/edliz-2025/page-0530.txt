Unfractionated heparin is given subcutaneously or intravenously. The dose is
dependent on the activated partial thromboplastin time ratio (aPTTR). It has a
short half-life and needs laboratory facilities.
Low molecular weight heparin is given subcutaneously with a fixed dose. No
laboratory monitoring facilities are required.
