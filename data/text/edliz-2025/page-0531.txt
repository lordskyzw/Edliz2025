Prophylaxis for Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)
This is indicated for all patients who have high risk factors for thrombosis before
and after surgery. These conditions include:
@ Obesity
e Prolonged immobility
e Hereditary thrombophilia states (antithrombin III, factor V Leiden deficiency,
protein C & S deficiency etc.)
e Paroxysmal nocturnal haemoglobinuria
e Previous history of DVT
e Various malignancies
Other pro-thrombotic states such as artificial cardiac valves and atrial fibrillation
need lifelong anticoagulation.
Methods of prophylaxis available
Physical methods include stockings. Early mobility must be encouraged in all
surgical patients.
Medicine management (targeting an INR of 2 to 2.5):
