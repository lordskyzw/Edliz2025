SKIN CONTAMINATION

Many chemicals can be absorbed through skin or cause direct injury to the skin.

e Wash with large quantities of cold water. Avoid hypothermia.

e Do not delay in removing clothing - this can be done while the skin is being
washed.

e After removal of any contaminated clothing continue thorough washing with
large amount of cold water and soap (including hair if contaminated).

e Avoid contaminating yourself.

EYE CONTAMINATION

See also chapter on Common Eye Conditions.

e The eyelids should be held apart and the eye washed with a gentle stream of
water (e.g. from tap, hose pipe, or jug) for at least 15 minutes.

e Protect the unaffected eye.

TREATMENT OF SPECIFIC POISONINGS

Antidepressants

E.g. amitriptyline and imipramine (tricyclic antidepressants). Signs of poisoning

with these medicines are CNS stimulation and cardiac arrhythmias.

e Establish airway and maintain respiration. Monitor ECG until the patient is free
of arrhythmia for 24 hours.

e Remove ingested medicine by gastric lavage followed by activated charcoal. Do
not induce emesis since patients may become comatose rapidly.

e Maintain blood pressure by giving intravenous fluid. Avoid vasoconstrictor
agents.

e Control convulsions by giving:
