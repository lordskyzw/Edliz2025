Aspirin / Salicylate Poisoning
Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) is present in many analgesic preparations, and may
also be found in herbal medicines. The toxic dose of any salicylate is estimated to
be 0.2-0.5 g/kg.
EMERGENCY MEASURES
e Delay absorption of the poison by giving activated charcoal. If respiration is
depressed, use airway-protected gastric lavage (lavage is effective up to 4 hours
after ingestion).
e If blood pressure is low, treat appropriately.
e Treat respiratory depression by administering oxygen. Artificial ventilation may
be necessary.
e If convulsions occur and hypoglycaemia is not a contributing factor, give
anticonvulsant medicine.
Caution: Central nervous system depressants, such as barbiturates or diazepam
must be administered cautiously.
GENERAL MEASURES
e Monitor serum bicarbonate, chloride, potassium, sodium, glucose and arterial
pH.
e Ifthere is adequate urine output and no vomiting.
e In severe poisoning, hydration with intravenous fluids must be initiated in the
first hour:
