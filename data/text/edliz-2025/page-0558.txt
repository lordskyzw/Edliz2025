Antidote
The antidote is effective if given up to 36hrs after ingestion.
If it is suspected that the person has taken in excess of 10 g (20 tablets of 500 mg
each) or if the 4 hour plasma paracetamol level exceeds 150 mg/ml administration
of antidote is recommended:
Medicine Codes Adult dose Frequency Duration
acetylcysteine iv A V__ 150mg/kg in 200ml over 1 5mins, then
infusion in dextrose 50mg/kg in 500ml over 4hrs, then
5% 100mg/kg in 1000ml over 1 6hrs
ETHANOL (ALCOHOL) POISONING
e Remove unabsorbed ethanol by gastric lavage (note that activated charcoal does
not absorb alcohol) if performed soon after ingestion
e Maintain adequate airway. Give artificial respiration if necessary.
