Propofol
Produces anaesthesia but no analgesia in one arm-brain circulation time. Recovery
is rapid with minimal post-op nausea and vomiting.
Medicine Codes Dose Onset Duration
propofol iv A V_ 2-2.5mg/kg 40sec 5-10min
Indications
e Induction of general anaesthesia
e Conscious sedation
e Maintenance of anaesthesia.
Caution
e Check manufacturer’s storage conditions specifications ( some brands should be
refrigerated and some should be stored at room temperature)
e Reduce dose in the elderly and high-risk patients.
e Minimize pain by injecting into large vein and/or mixing with lignocaine
e Avoid in children less than one year and epileptic patients
e In patients for Caesarean Section
e Discard unused solutions
e Contraindicated in people allergic to eggs and soya bean oil
Ketamine
Produces dissociative anaesthesia gradually, in high risk or hypovolaemic patients.
Do not use in patients with decompensated shock as it is a direct myocardial
depressant.
