Atracurium Besylate (non-depolarising)
An intermediate acting benzylsoquinolium non-depolarizing muscle relaxant that
undergoes Hofmann elimination and non-specific enzymatic ester hydrolysis. It is
thus safe to use in patients with significant renal or hepatic failure.
