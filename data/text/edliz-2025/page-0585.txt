Medicines Used in Shock
Shock could be caused by the following:
e Acute pump failure — myocardial infarction, arrhythmias, valve rupture
e Volume loss (hypovolaemia) —haemorrhage, dehydration
e Loss of vascular tone- septic shock, neurogenic shock, endocrine failure,
anaphylactic shock
It is important to make an accurate aetiologic diagnosis for appropriate treatment.
Ideally CVP monitoring is required.
